Flux traces are PDCSAP light curves detrended with a biweight filter, bucket-averaged to ~260 points for display. Period, depth, SDE and SNR come from a Transit Least Squares periodogram search — a raw scan of every processed candidate, not a vetted catalog. Real light curves are TESS Sector 1 SPOC 2-minute cadence data downloaded from MAST; synthetic entries are batman-modeled transit injections used to balance training classes.
